Detection Engine

4 manipulation
patterns. All caught.

Statistical anomaly detection with sigma scoring, cancel ratio analysis, and timing-based classification.

Layering

Order Book Manipulation

Trader T-1042 placed 14 large BUY orders on HDFCBANK creating fake demand. Cancelled 12 within 612ms after price moved up, then sold real shares at elevated price.

Cancel ratio: 85.7% (baseline: 15%)
Anomaly: 14.14σ above normal
Trigger: cancel_ratio > 70% + sell executed
Spoofing

Ghost Order Placement

Trader T-2891 placed an 80,000-share RELIANCE order worth ₹10.3 crore, then cancelled it in 420ms — algorithmically timed price manipulation.

Cancel time: 420ms (human-impossible)
Order value: ₹10.3 crore notional
Trigger: size > 50k + cancel_ms < 600
Wash Trading

Self-Dealing Volume Fraud

Trader T-3301 bought 10,000 INFY shares on Account A and sold identical quantity on Account B within 18 seconds — creating artificial market activity.

Same trader, two accounts
Time window: 18 seconds
Trigger: size diff < 10% within 30s
Pump & Dump

Price Inflation Scheme

Trader T-4401 accumulated 110,000 TCS shares quietly over 14 minutes, then dumped everything within 8 minutes at an elevated price — leaving other investors stranded.

Accumulation: 110,000 shares / 14min
Dump window: 8 minutes
Trigger: >100k shares + sell < 10min
